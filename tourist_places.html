🇮🇳 Top 10 Tourist Places in India 🇮🇳

Explore the most beautiful and historic destinations across India

1Taj Mahal - Agra

The Taj Mahal is one of the most beautiful monuments in the world and a symbol of love. Built by Emperor Shah Jahan for his wife Mumtaz Mahal, it is made of white marble and took 22 years to complete.

State: Uttar Pradesh

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: ₹250 (Indian), ₹600 (Foreigner)

2Gateway of India - Mumbai

An iconic monument and proud landmark of Mumbai. Built in 1924, this yellow basalt stone structure welcomes visitors to the financial capital of India with its architectural brilliance.

State: Maharashtra

Best Time to Visit: November to February

Entry Fee: Free

3Statue of Unity - Vadodara

The world's tallest statue standing at 182 meters, dedicated to Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel. Located in Gujarat, it offers panoramic views of the Narmada Valley.

State: Gujarat

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: ₹350 (Adult)

4Hawa Mahal - Jaipur

Known as the "Palace of Winds," this magnificent pink structure is a must-visit in Jaipur. Built in 1799, it features 953 small windows designed to allow royal women to watch the streets unobserved.

State: Rajasthan

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: ₹250 (Indian), ₹400 (Foreigner)

5Varanasi - Holy City

One of the holiest cities in Hinduism, Varanasi is situated on the banks of the Ganges River. Famous for its spiritual significance, temples, and stunning ghats, it attracts millions of pilgrims annually.

State: Uttar Pradesh

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: Free (Temple visits)

6Kedarnath Temple - Uttarakhand

A sacred Hindu pilgrimage site located at an altitude of 3,583 meters. Dedicated to Lord Shiva, it is one of the four main pilgrimage sites (Char Dham) in India.

State: Uttarakhand

Best Time to Visit: May to September

Entry Fee: Free

7Darjeeling - Tea Garden Paradise

Located in the foothills of the Himalayas, Darjeeling is famous for its tea gardens and stunning views of Kanchenjunga mountain. The toy train journey is an unforgettable experience.

State: West Bengal

Best Time to Visit: March to May & September to October

Entry Fee: Varies by attraction

8Kanyakumari - The Three Seas

The southernmost tip of mainland India where three seas meet. Famous for its stunning sunset views, Vivekananda Rock Memorial, and rich cultural heritage.

State: Tamil Nadu

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: ₹50 (Indian), ₹250 (Foreigner)

9Goa - Beach Paradise

Known for its pristine beaches, vibrant nightlife, Portuguese heritage, and water sports. Goa is a perfect destination for relaxation and adventure seekers alike.

State: Goa

Best Time to Visit: November to February

Entry Fee: Free (Beaches)

10Hampi - Ancient Ruins

A UNESCO World Heritage Site with ancient temples and ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire. Located along the Tungabhadra River, Hampi is a paradise for history and archaeology enthusiasts.

State: Karnataka

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Entry Fee: ₹250 (Indian), ₹500 (Foreigner)
